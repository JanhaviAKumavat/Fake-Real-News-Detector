About Fake News Detector AI

Fighting misinformation using Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Natural Language Processing.

Our Project

Fake News Detector AI is an intelligent web application that identifies whether a news article is genuine or fake using Machine Learning algorithms.

The system analyzes textual information, extracts meaningful features using Natural Language Processing (NLP), and predicts the authenticity of the news with high confidence.

Our goal is to help people verify information before believing or sharing misleading content online.
